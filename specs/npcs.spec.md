# NPC Specification — Personalities, Companions & Disposition

## Overview

This document defines the named NPCs of the Tile World RPG, their personalities,
speech patterns, faction loyalties, and mechanical disposition system. It also
specifies the OSRIC henchmen and hireling system for player companions.

Named NPCs are persistent characters with fixed locations, personalities, and
agendas. They are not procedurally generated — each is hand-authored to serve
a narrative and mechanical purpose. Procedurally generated townsfolk and
merchants are covered separately in the disposition mechanics section.

## 1. Named NPCs by Region

### Veluna & Golden Plains (Phase 1)

#### Elder Thessa Brighthearth

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Location | Veluna, Cathedral of the True Light |
| Role | Quest-giver, healer, spiritual guide |
| Faction | Western Alliance (Veluna) |
| Disposition | Friendly (default) |
| Level equivalent | Cleric 5 |

**Personality:** Warm but unsentimental. Thessa has buried too many young
adventurers to be romantic about the profession. She values preparation over
courage and says so plainly. She has served the Cathedral for thirty years and
knows every road, ruin, and rumor within fifty miles.

**Speech pattern:** Direct, maternal without condescension. Uses agricultural
metaphors. Never raises her voice.

**Sample dialogue:**
- "You'll want rations before you want a sword. The Stonefell doesn't care how brave you are."
- "The Northern Road patrol came back short two scouts. Something in the Silver Leaf Wood is hunting back."
- "Pray if it helps you. But sharpen your blade first."

**Quest connections:** Shrine of the Earth Mother (Phase 1), The Sunstone
Question (Phase 2, gives the player background on Solar Invokers).

#### Captain Aldren Holt

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Location | Bordos, Border Watch Tower |
| Role | Military contact, bounty-giver |
| Faction | Shield Lands Coalition |
| Disposition | Neutral → Friendly (after first bounty completed) |
| Level equivalent | Fighter 4 |

**Personality:** Exhausted professional. Holt has been posted at Bordos for
three years with inadequate troops and no reinforcements. He is pragmatic to
the point of bluntness and does not trust adventurers until they prove
themselves. Once trust is earned, he is fiercely loyal.

**Speech pattern:** Military brevity. Reports facts, not feelings. Numbers and
directions. Swears under stress.

**Sample dialogue:**
- "Fourteen patrols this month. Eleven came back. Those numbers don't work."
- "I don't need heroes. I need someone who follows orders and comes back alive."
- "The Horned Society doesn't send scouts for fun. Something's coming. I can feel it in the reports."

**Quest connections:** Scout the Northern Road (Phase 1), Defend the Shield
Wall (Phase 2), provides intelligence for Shield Lands quests.

#### Mira Copperkettle

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Location | Veluna, Crossroads Market |
| Role | Merchant, rumor source |
| Faction | None (independent) |
| Disposition | Friendly |
| Level equivalent | Commoner 2 |

**Personality:** Cheerful, shrewd, and talkative. Mira runs a general goods
stall and hears everything that passes through the crossroads. She gossips
freely but her information is reliable — she has been burned by bad rumors
before and now checks her sources.

**Speech pattern:** Fast, conversational, asks questions back. Uses coin
metaphors.

**Sample dialogue:**
- "Grain wagon from Glintshore never showed up. That's two in a row. Someone's eating well on the road."
- "You heading north? Buy an extra waterskin. The wells past Ioz taste like sulfur."
- "I'll give you eight silver for the lot. Don't look at me like that — I'm the only buyer between here and Urnst."

**Quest connections:** Escort the Grain Wagon (Phase 1), provides market
price information and trade route rumors.

### Amber Plains Hegemony (Phase 2-3)

#### Grand Gatekeeper Vance

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Location | Crown-Hold, Gatekeeper's Citadel |
| Role | Faction leader, quest-giver, political actor |
| Faction | Amber Plains Hegemony |
| Disposition | Neutral (transactional) |
| Level equivalent | Fighter 9 |

**Personality:** Cold, calculating, and absolutely incorruptible — at least by
money. Vance's currency is loyalty and results. He built Crown-Hold's toll
system into a continental chokepoint and takes personal offense at anyone who
circumvents it. His obsession with order borders on tyranny, but his people
are well-fed and his walls have never been breached.

**Speech pattern:** Clipped, contractual. States terms, not requests. Never
says please. Uses silence as a weapon.

**Sample dialogue:**
- "Twelve percent. Every caravan. Every coin. That's how the walls stay up."
- "My daughter chose piracy over duty. That tells you everything about the state of the world."
- "The catacombs beneath the Spine are Rossi's lifeline. Cut it, and I'll remember your name."

**Quest connections:** The Syndicate Siphon (Phase 2), The Gatekeeper's
Vendetta (Phase 3), Drake Plague intelligence.

#### Sylvia Rossi

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Location | Glintstore, The Underwarrens (hidden) |
| Role | Syndicate leader, black market access |
| Faction | Glintstore Syndicates (opposed to Crown-Hold) |
| Disposition | Hostile → Neutral (if player proves useful) |
| Level equivalent | Thief 7 |

**Personality:** Patient, amused, and dangerous. Rossi runs the largest
smuggling operation on the continent from a network of tunnels beneath
Glintstore. She genuinely believes the Spine Toll is economic tyranny and
sees herself as a liberator, not a criminal. She tests everyone who enters
her network with escalating tasks before revealing anything important.

**Speech pattern:** Soft-spoken, uses questions instead of statements. Never
threatens directly — implies consequences.

**Sample dialogue:**
- "The Gatekeeper charges twelve percent to cross a mountain. I charge nothing. Who's the thief?"
- "You want access to my tunnels? First, prove you can keep a secret. Then prove you're useful. Then we'll talk."
- "Vance thinks order is a wall. I think it's a river. Water always finds a way through."

**Quest connections:** The Syndicate Siphon (Phase 2, alternate path — help
Rossi instead of Vance), provides black market equipment and information.

### Frozen Coalition (Phase 3)

#### Overseer Boros

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Location | Ashtown, Central Foundry |
| Role | Labor leader, political actor |
| Faction | Ashtown Workers' Council |
| Disposition | Suspicious → Neutral (if player shows class solidarity) |
| Level equivalent | Fighter 6 |

**Personality:** Angry, articulate, and exhausted. Boros has spent twenty years
in the foundries and watched three generations of workers die from lung-rot
while Borealis shamans live in heated domes. He organizes the strike not from
ideology but from desperation — his people are dying and no one in the capital
cares. He distrusts southerners profoundly but will accept help if it comes
without strings.

**Speech pattern:** Blunt, uses collective pronouns ("we," "our people"),
speaks in short declarative sentences. Voice is hoarse from years of ash
inhalation.

**Sample dialogue:**
- "We dig the ore. We smelt the iron. We breathe the ash. And Borealis gets the heat."
- "You're from the south. That means you want something. Say it plain or get out of my foundry."
- "Forty-eight hours. That's how long Borealis lasts without our heat pipes. They know it. We know it. Time to negotiate."

**Quest connections:** The Ashtown Industrial Strike (Phase 3).

#### High Shaman Kael

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Location | Borealis, Ice Dome Sanctum |
| Role | Faction leader, spiritual authority |
| Faction | Frozen Coalition (Borealis government) |
| Disposition | Cold → Neutral (if player demonstrates respect for northern customs) |
| Level equivalent | Cleric 8 |

**Personality:** Regal, patient, and deeply conservative. Kael speaks for the
ancestral spirits and carries the weight of Coalition tradition. He views
Ashtown's grievances as legitimate but believes the strike will destroy the
Coalition entirely, leaving both cities vulnerable to southern exploitation.
He is not cruel — he simply values the whole over the parts.

**Speech pattern:** Measured, formal, uses ancestral proverbs. Long pauses
between sentences. Never interrupts.

**Sample dialogue:**
- "The ice does not negotiate. It endures or it breaks. We must endure."
- "Boros speaks for the living. I speak for the dead and the unborn. Their needs are different."
- "You carry southern warmth in your voice. That is not an insult. But do not mistake our cold for weakness."

**Quest connections:** The Ashtown Industrial Strike (Phase 3, opposing
perspective to Boros), The Whispering Spire Malfunction.

### Golden Vales Confederation (Phase 3)

#### High Archivist Morwenna

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Location | Prelacia, Grand Archive |
| Role | Scholar, lore source, quest-giver |
| Faction | Prelacia (politically neutral) |
| Disposition | Friendly (to seekers of knowledge) |
| Level equivalent | Magic-User 7 |

**Personality:** Brilliant, obsessive, and terrified of her own discovery.
Morwenna found the Forbidden Scroll six months ago and has been unable to
sleep properly since. She knows the information could prevent tyranny or
cause a civil war, and she cannot decide which outcome is more likely. She
seeks an outsider — someone without factional loyalty — to help her decide.

**Speech pattern:** Precise, academic, uses qualifiers and citations. Speaks
faster when nervous. References texts by catalog number.

**Sample dialogue:**
- "The scroll dates to the second century post-Charter. The calligraphy is authentic. The seal is the High Duke's own — or rather, his predecessor's."
- "I am not asking you to decide for me. I am asking you to understand what is at stake before I decide."
- "Prelacia swore neutrality. I swore neutrality. But neutrality in the face of fraud is its own kind of crime."

**Quest connections:** The Forbidden Scroll (Phase 3).

### Theocracy of Sunstone (Phase 2-3)

#### Solar Invoker Castiel

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Location | Veluna (Phase 2), mobile (Phase 3) |
| Role | Diplomat, spy, potential antagonist or ally |
| Faction | Theocracy of Sunstone |
| Disposition | Neutral (strategic) |
| Level equivalent | Cleric 6 |

**Personality:** Charming, devout, and genuinely believes in the Solar
Ascendancy's mission. Castiel is not a fanatic — he is an idealist, which is
more dangerous. He sees the western realms as suffering under secular chaos
and sincerely offers Sunstone's protection. His offer comes with strings that
tighten slowly.

**Speech pattern:** Formal, warm, uses light metaphors. Never argues — only
offers and waits. Smiles when he should not.

**Sample dialogue:**
- "The Light offers warmth to all who seek it. We ask only that you face the sun."
- "Your independence is admirable. But independence without protection is just another word for vulnerability."
- "I do not threaten. I illuminate. What you see in the light is your own concern."

**Quest connections:** The Sunstone Question (Phase 2), Eclipse Conspiracy
(Phase 3, can be turned or eliminated).

### Obsidian Sea (Phase 3)

#### Captain Valeria Vance

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Location | Gradsul, The Corsair's Rest |
| Role | Privateer captain, potential ally |
| Faction | Obsidian Sea (independent pirate) |
| Disposition | Hostile → Neutral → Friendly (earned through action) |
| Level equivalent | Fighter 7 |

**Personality:** Fierce, pragmatic, and haunted by her break with her father.
Valeria left Crown-Hold at seventeen after watching Gatekeeper Vance execute
a toll-dodger's entire caravan guard — twelve men, over sixteen Aureus of
undeclared spice. She stole a ship and never went back. She is a pirate by
necessity and a revolutionary by temperament.

**Speech pattern:** Naval slang, dark humor, tests everyone's nerve. Swears
freely. Laughs at danger.

**Sample dialogue:**
- "My father counts coins. I count waves. We both know what's coming, but only one of us is honest about it."
- "You want passage through the Cinder Straits? Fine. But you pull your weight on deck or you swim."
- "The Dawn Legion burns everything it touches. The Sea Princes? We just take a cut."

**Quest connections:** The Gatekeeper's Vendetta (Phase 3, target or ally),
Obsidian Armament (provides ship access), Xanatos Expedition (naval transport).

### Serpentine Jungle (Phase 3)

#### Matriarch Na'Zala

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Location | Ruby Citadel, Canopy Throne |
| Role | Tribal leader, quest-giver |
| Faction | Serpentine Jungle Tribes |
| Disposition | Hostile → Neutral (if player proves opposition to Sunstone and loggers) |
| Level equivalent | Druid 8 (equivalent) |

**Personality:** Ancient, furious, and heartbroken. Na'Zala has watched
northern empires strip the jungle's edges for two generations. She has lost
three daughters to Sunstone slaving expeditions. Her hatred is specific and
justified. She does not trust outsiders but recognizes that the Defiler's
Fortress requires skills her guerrillas lack.

**Speech pattern:** Minimal. Long silences. When she speaks, every word
carries weight. Nature metaphors that sound like threats.

**Sample dialogue:**
- "The trees are patient. I am not."
- "Your northern empires call us savages. We call you a disease. The difference is, diseases can be cured."
- "The blood-gems remember everything. Every tree cut. Every child taken. The jungle keeps its debts."

**Quest connections:** The Defiler's Fortress (Phase 3), The Rotting Heart
(Phase 3).

## 2. Disposition Mechanics

### Disposition Scale

Every NPC has a disposition toward the player, tracked as an integer:

| Range | Label | Effect |
|-------|-------|--------|
| -100 to -61 | Sworn Enemy | Attacks on sight; sends assassins |
| -60 to -21 | Hostile | Refuses interaction; may attack if provoked |
| -20 to -1 | Unfriendly | Terse; inflated prices (+50%); limited dialogue |
| 0 to 20 | Neutral | Standard interaction; standard prices |
| 21 to 60 | Friendly | Expanded dialogue; fair prices; shares rumors |
| 61 to 100 | Allied | Discounted prices (-20%); offers aid; shares secrets |

### Disposition Modifiers

| Action | Modifier |
|--------|----------|
| Complete quest for NPC | +15 to +25 |
| Complete quest for NPC's enemy | -10 to -20 |
| Gift (valuable item) | +5 to +15 |
| CHA reaction roll (2d6 + CHA mod) | +/- per OSRIC reaction table |
| Theft detected | -30 |
| Attack NPC's faction member | -40 |
| Save NPC's life | +30 |
| Betray NPC's trust | -50 |
| Regional reputation (completed quests in area) | +2 per quest |

### Faction Disposition

Faction standing applies as a baseline modifier to all NPCs of that faction:

```
effective_disposition = personal_disposition + faction_standing
```

A player with -40 faction standing with Sunstone starts every Sunstone NPC
interaction at -40 before personal modifiers.

### Disposition Persistence

- Named NPCs: disposition stored in `tile_npcs` (permanent, per-character)
- Procedural NPCs: disposition computed from faction standing + CHA reaction
  roll (rolled once per NPC, stored if the NPC is interacted with)
- Faction standing: stored in a `character_factions` table (one row per
  faction per character)

## 3. NPC Reaction Rolls (OSRIC)

When meeting a new NPC whose disposition is not predetermined:

Roll 2d6 + CHA modifier:

| Roll | Reaction |
|------|----------|
| 2 or less | Hostile — attacks or flees |
| 3-5 | Unfriendly — refuses aid, may demand toll |
| 6-8 | Neutral — cautious, will trade but not volunteer |
| 9-11 | Friendly — helpful, shares information |
| 12+ | Enthusiastic — offers aid, may join as hireling |

Modifiers:
- Racial prejudice: -2 for half-orc in human lands, +1 for elf in elven lands
- Regional reputation: +1 per 3 completed quests in the region
- Faction standing: applied as described above

## 4. Companion System — Henchmen & Hirelings

### Henchmen (OSRIC)

Henchmen are named NPC companions who travel with the player. They are
recruited through roleplay, not purchased.

| Property | Rule |
|----------|------|
| Maximum henchmen | CHA-based (see ability score table) |
| Level | Always 1-3 levels below the player |
| Loyalty | Base 50% + CHA loyalty modifier; checked in crisis |
| XP share | Henchmen receive half XP (split from party total) |
| Equipment | Player must equip them; they use standard combat rules |
| Death | Permanent; lowers party morale; may trigger loyalty checks in other henchmen |

### Loyalty Checks

Roll d100 against loyalty score when:
- The henchman takes 50%+ HP damage in one hit
- The player orders a clearly suicidal action
- The player betrays an ally in the henchman's presence
- The henchman hasn't been paid in 30 game days

Failed loyalty: henchman refuses the order, deserts, or (at very low loyalty)
betrays the player.

### Hiring Process

1. Player visits inn or tavern in a settlement
2. Available henchmen are generated based on settlement size and region
3. Player makes a CHA reaction roll to recruit
4. Recruited henchman starts with basic equipment and loyalty 50% + CHA mod
5. Henchman has a name, class, level, and personality trait

### Henchman Personality Traits

Each henchman has one dominant trait that affects their behavior:

| Trait | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Brave | +10 loyalty in combat; volunteers for danger |
| Cautious | -10 loyalty for reckless orders; warns of traps |
| Greedy | +5 loyalty when paid extra; -15 if loot is unfairly split |
| Loyal | +20 base loyalty; never betrays (but may still desert) |
| Ambitious | -5 loyalty per level gap >2; may challenge for leadership |
| Devout | +10 loyalty if party serves their deity; -20 for desecration |
| Pragmatic | No loyalty bonuses or penalties; purely transactional |

### Hirelings

Hirelings are unnamed, temporary workers hired for specific tasks:

| Type | Cost (per day) | Service |
|------|---------------|---------|
| Torchbearer | 1 sp | Carries light source, 0 combat ability |
| Porter | 2 sp | Carries 100 lbs of equipment |
| Guide | 5 sp | Eliminates lost chance in specific terrain |
| Man-at-arms | 2 gp | Light armor, spear, follows orders in combat |
| Sergeant | 5 gp | Commands up to 10 men-at-arms |

Hirelings do not gain XP, do not have loyalty scores (they simply leave if
not paid), and are not tracked individually by name.

## 5. Vendor NPCs

### Vendor Mechanics

NPCs with `vendor = true` in `tile_npcs` can buy and sell items:

| Property | Rule |
|----------|------|
| Buy price | 50% of item base cost (standard) |
| Sell price | 100% of item base cost (modified by disposition) |
| Disposition discount | Allied: -20%; Friendly: -10%; Neutral: 0%; Unfriendly: +50% |
| Inventory | Seeded by settlement size and region (deterministic from tile address) |
| Restocking | Vendors restock inventory every 7 game days |

### Specialist Vendors

| Vendor Type | Items Available | Typical Location |
|-------------|----------------|-----------------|
| General merchant | Rations, rope, torches, basic gear | Any settlement |
| Weaponsmith | Weapons, ammunition | Towns with smithy |
| Armorsmith | Armor, shields | Towns with smithy |
| Alchemist | Potions, antidotes, oils | Large towns, Volcano's Edge |
| Scribe | Scrolls, spellbooks, maps | Prelacia, Hinwanit |
| Horse trader | Mounts, saddles, feed | Plains settlements |
| Fence | Stolen goods, contraband (no questions) | Gradsul, Glintstore |
| Exotic trader | Regional specialties, rare components | Port cities, Crown-Hold |

## 6. NPC Schedule & Behavior

### Daily Schedule

Named NPCs follow a simple daily schedule:

| Time | Location |
|------|----------|
| Dawn - Morning | At their post (shop, temple, barracks, etc.) |
| Midday | Break location (inn, market, home) |
| Afternoon | At their post |
| Evening | Inn or home |
| Night | Home (unavailable for interaction) |

### NPC Absence

If a named NPC is not at their expected location:
- A subordinate or sign explains the absence
- Quest-critical NPCs are never absent during their quest's active phase
- NPCs may travel between locations as the campaign progresses

## 7. NPC Data Model Extension

The existing `tile_npcs` table supports basic NPC storage. Named NPCs with
rich personality data require additional fields tracked in the game's
narrative layer:

| Field | Type | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| npc_id | TEXT | Unique identifier (e.g., "elder_thessa") |
| region | TEXT | Home region for speech pattern lookup |
| faction_id | TEXT | FK to faction for standing calculations |
| personality_trait | TEXT | Dominant trait for dialogue selection |
| quest_connections | TEXT | Comma-separated quest IDs this NPC is involved in |
| phase_available | INTEGER | Campaign phase when this NPC becomes active |

These fields can be stored as additional columns on `tile_npcs` or as a
separate `named_npcs` reference table that `tile_npcs` rows link to via
`npc_id`.
