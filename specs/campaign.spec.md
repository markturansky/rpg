# Campaign Specification — Story Arcs & Quest Sequencing

## Overview

This document defines the narrative arc of the Tile World RPG, organized into
five phases mapped to OSRIC level ranges. The player spawns near Veluna at
level 1 and encounters escalating regional, continental, and apocalyptic
threats as they advance.

Each phase specifies:
- Level range and geographic scope
- Canon story beats (events that occur regardless of player action)
- Player-driven quest lines (branching based on choices)
- Faction escalation (how the world changes around the player)
- Transition trigger (what ends this phase and begins the next)

The save state always overrides. The game never assumes where the player is
in the story — it reads their position, level, faction standings, and
completed quests to determine which phase content is active.

---

## Phase 1 — Local Threats (Levels 1–3)

### Geographic Scope

Veluna and the surrounding Golden Plains, Stonefell Rocks, and Shield Lands
border. Roughly a 6-tile radius from the spawn point at `map2_35`.

### Canon Beats

These events happen on a timer or trigger regardless of player action:

1. **Bandit raids intensify** — Merchant caravans on the Central Trade Way
   start arriving damaged or not at all. Veluna's market prices rise 20%.
2. **Shield Lands call for aid** — Town Hall posts bounties for humanoid
   patrols sighted south of the Silver Leaf Wood.
3. **Horned Society probe** — A dead scout is found on the road north of
   Bordos carrying a sigil of the Horned Society. Rangers at Ioz confirm
   increased activity.

### Player Quest Lines

| Quest | Source | Type | Reward | Notes |
|-------|--------|------|--------|-------|
| Clear the Stonefell Kobolds | Veluna Town Hall | Kill | 100 gp + 200 XP | Introduces dungeon crawling |
| Escort the Grain Wagon | Veluna Market | Escort | 75 gp + 150 XP | Road to Glintshore; bandit ambush |
| The Missing Prospector | Glintshore Prospector's Guild | Fetch | 150 gp + 300 XP | Stonefell Rocks cave; harpy nest |
| Scout the Northern Road | Ioz Ranger Station | Exploration | 200 gp + 400 XP | First glimpse of Horned Society territory |
| Shrine of the Earth Mother | Cathedral of the True Light | Fetch | Free healing + Bless scroll | Retrieve relic from Sumalen Land bogs |

### Faction Standing

| Faction | Starting Disposition | Notes |
|---------|---------------------|-------|
| Veluna | Friendly | Home base; full services |
| Shield Lands | Neutral | Aware of player if bounties completed |
| Bandit Kingdoms | Hostile | Always hostile |
| Horned Society | Unknown | Player doesn't yet know they exist |
| Golden Vales | Indifferent | Lorenstadt is distant and irrelevant at this level |

### Tone

Discovery and grounding. The player learns the world's rhythms — day/night
cycles, terrain navigation, town services, basic combat. Danger is local
and comprehensible. NPCs are warm and direct. The world feels large but
the player's corner of it feels manageable.

### Transition Trigger

Level 4 reached, OR the Horned Society probe quest completed. The player
receives a summons from the Shield Lands requesting experienced adventurers.

## Phase 2 — Regional Conflict (Levels 4–6)

### Geographic Scope

Expands to include the Shield Lands, Bandit Kingdoms border, Silver Leaf
Wood, Lake Aegean, and the Duchy of Urnst. The Amber Plains Hegemony
becomes relevant as a trade partner and information source.

### Canon Beats

1. **Horned Society mobilization** — Humanoid warbands pour south from the
   dark forests. Shield Lands frontier settlements are besieged.
2. **Golden Vales grain embargo** — Lorenstadt restricts grain exports to
   pressure the Amber Plains, causing food shortages across the western
   realms. Veluna's market prices double.
3. **The Drake Plague begins** — Spine Drakes in the Amber Plains start
   dying. Gatekeeper Vance sends emissaries seeking alchemical expertise.
4. **Sunstone missionary arrives** — A Solar Invoker appears in Veluna
   offering "protection" in exchange for tribute. The town council is split.

### Player Quest Lines

| Quest | Source | Type | Reward | Notes |
|-------|--------|------|--------|-------|
| Defend the Shield Wall | Shield Lands fortress | Kill | 500 gp + 800 XP | Multi-wave defense against humanoid assault |
| Infiltrate the Bandit Camp | Shield Lands commander | Kill/Fetch | 600 gp + 1000 XP + map | Locate Horned Society supply cache |
| The Syndicate Siphon | Gatekeeper Vance (Crown-Hold) | Exploration | 800 gp + Aureus currency access | Explore catacombs beneath the Draconic Spine |
| The Drake Plague Cure | Volcano's Edge alchemists | Fetch | 700 gp + Drake-Rider ally | Gather ingredients from Silver Leaf Wood and Stonefell |
| Silver Leaf Wood Patrol | Shield Lands ranger captain | Exploration | 400 gp + 600 XP | Map deep forest; encounter treants and wood elf scouts |
| The Sunstone Question | Veluna town council | Diplomacy | Faction standing shift | Accept, reject, or negotiate the Solar Invoker's offer |

### Faction Standing Shifts

| Event | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Defend Shield Lands | Shield Lands → Allied; Bandit Kingdoms → Sworn Enemy |
| Complete Syndicate Siphon | Amber Plains Hegemony → Friendly; Glintstore syndicates → Hostile |
| Cure Drake Plague | Amber Plains → Allied; Spine Drake-Riders available as allies |
| Accept Sunstone tribute | Sunstone → Favorable; Western Alliance → Suspicious |
| Reject Sunstone tribute | Sunstone → Hostile; Western Alliance → Friendly |

### Tone

Escalation and consequence. The player's choices start having political
weight. NPCs reference the player's earlier actions. The world's factions
begin pulling the player in competing directions. Combat encounters are
deadlier — mixed humanoid squads, spellcasting enemies, terrain hazards.

### Transition Trigger

Level 7 reached, OR two or more faction standings reach Allied/Hostile.
A continental crisis event fires (see Phase 3 triggers).

## Phase 3 — Continental Power Struggles (Levels 7–8)

### Geographic Scope

The entire continent opens. The player has reason to visit every macro-region:
the Frozen Coalition (Dark Iron diplomacy), the Golden Vales (political
intrigue), the Theocracy of Sunstone (infiltration or alliance), the
Serpentine Jungle (blood-gem research), and the Obsidian Sea (obsidian
weapons procurement).

### Canon Beats

1. **The Ashtown Industrial Strike** — Overseer Boros shuts down the
   geothermal heat pipes. Borealis faces 48-hour freeze-out. The Frozen
   Coalition fractures.
2. **The Freehold Grain Strike** — Vitalia basin farmers rebel against
   Lorenstadt. Sunstone spies are funding the rebellion. The Golden Vales
   Confederation teeters.
3. **Solar Eclipse approaches** — Arcane scholars calculate a total eclipse
   within months. Sunstone's Prism Towers and Prismatic Sentinels will go
   dark. Secular rebels begin recruiting.
4. **Xanatos rising** — Tectonic activity raises a section of the sunken
   Ruins of Xanatos above sea level. Every major navy mobilizes.
5. **The Whispering Spire** — North-Watch's acoustic warning system inverts,
   luring rime-beasts toward the fortress. Something is manipulating the
   north.

### Player Quest Lines

| Quest | Source | Type | Reward | Notes |
|-------|--------|------|--------|-------|
| Negotiate the Ashtown Crisis | Borealis High Shaman | Diplomacy | Dark Iron supply alliance | Broker peace or pick a side |
| The Forbidden Scroll | High Archivist Morwenna (Prelacia) | Fetch/Diplomacy | Political leverage | Proof the High Duke's lineage is illegitimate |
| Eclipse Conspiracy | Secular rebel network | Kill/Infiltration | Solar-shard technology | Infiltrate Solis-Prime during the eclipse |
| Xanatos Expedition | Any naval faction | Exploration | Ancient weapons/artifacts | Race other factions to the risen ruins |
| The Defiler's Fortress | Matriarch Na'Zala (Ruby Citadel) | Kill/Infiltration | Blood-gem alliance + jungle passage | Sabotage the logging syndicate in the Serpentine Jungle |
| Obsidian Armament | Basalt Council (Lavabury) | Fetch/Diplomacy | Obsidian weapons | Secure counter-Sunstone weaponry from the Sea Princes |
| The Rotting Heart | Ruby Citadel shamans | Fetch | Blood-gem mastery | Investigate the corrupting force beneath the tree-city |

### Continental Faction Web

The player's choices ripple across all factions:

| Player Action | Consequences |
|--------------|--------------|
| Side with Ashtown workers | Coalition military weakens; Dark Iron floods black market at low prices |
| Side with Borealis shamans | Ashtown suppressed; Coalition stabilizes but resentment festers |
| Reveal the Forbidden Scroll | Golden Vales civil war; Sunstone exploits the chaos |
| Suppress the Forbidden Scroll | High Duke survives; player gains powerful ally but moral cost |
| Join Eclipse Conspiracy | Sunstone weakens permanently; player becomes wanted by the Dawn Legion |
| Warn Sunstone of Eclipse plot | Sunstone alliance; rebels crushed; player gains Solar-shard tech |
| Reach Xanatos first | Ancient weapons; every other faction becomes hostile or demanding |

### Tone

Political complexity and moral ambiguity. There are no clean choices. Every
alliance costs something. The player is no longer a local adventurer — they
are a continental actor whose decisions reshape the power balance. Combat
is dangerous and consequential; death of allied NPCs is permanent.

### Transition Trigger

Level 9 reached (name level). The player is offered or claims a stronghold.
The continental crisis from Phase 3 resolves into one of several possible
Phase 4 configurations based on accumulated faction standings.

## Phase 4 — Name Level & Domain (Level 9)

### Geographic Scope

The player's stronghold tile and surrounding domain (2-tile radius, ~12 tiles)
plus continued continental engagement.

### Canon Beats

1. **Domain establishment** — The player must clear their claimed tiles of
   hostile encounters before construction begins (per game.spec.md §11).
2. **Followers arrive** — Class-specific followers appear per the name level
   table. They bring news of the continental situation.
3. **The world reacts** — Factions send emissaries to the player's stronghold.
   Allied factions offer trade agreements. Hostile factions probe defenses.
4. **Continental war looms** — The accumulated tensions from Phase 3 reach
   a breaking point. Whichever crisis the player left unresolved escalates
   to continental conflict.

### Player Activities

| Activity | System Reference | Notes |
|----------|-----------------|-------|
| Stronghold construction | game.spec.md §11 | Castle, tower, temple, or guild hall |
| Domain income management | game.spec.md §11 | Population growth, tax revenue, maintenance |
| Domain events (monthly) | game.spec.md §11 | 1d12 roll: plague, festivals, settlers, raids |
| Stronghold upgrades | game.spec.md §11 | Market, inn, walls, watchtower, smithy, etc. |
| Diplomatic reception | This document | Faction emissaries arrive; alliance or war |
| Mass combat | game.spec.md §11 | Defend domain against invading forces |

### Faction Emissary Table

Based on Phase 3 outcomes, the following emissaries may visit:

| Faction | If Allied | If Hostile |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Frozen Coalition | Trade Dark Iron at fair prices | Embargo; mercenary raids on supply lines |
| Amber Plains | Waive Spine Toll; open trade passes | Double toll; bounty hunters target player |
| Golden Vales | Military alliance; cavalry support | Grain embargo; knight patrols harass borders |
| Sunstone | Solar-shard technology sharing | Dawn Legion probing attacks; Solar Invoker spies |
| Obsidian Sea | Obsidian weapons at cost; privateer protection | Pirate raids on player's coastal trade |
| Serpentine Jungle | Blood-gem alliance; jungle passage | Viper Blowgunner assassination attempts |

### Tone

Responsibility and consequence. The player is no longer an adventurer — they
are a lord, guildmaster, high priest, or tower master. Every decision affects
their people. Domain events create emergent stories. The continental war
provides epic-scale stakes. Personal combat still matters but mass combat
and diplomacy become equally important.

### Transition Trigger

Stronghold fully constructed and domain population exceeds 1,000, OR
continental war breaks out. Either triggers Phase 5.

---

## Phase 5 — Continental War & Endgame (Level 9+)

### Geographic Scope

The entire continent. Multiple simultaneous fronts.

### Canon Beats

The specific war configuration depends on Phase 3 and 4 choices. The three
most likely scenarios:

**Scenario A — Sunstone Crusade:**
The Solar Archon declares holy war on the western realms. The Dawn Legion
marches west. The player must forge a continental alliance to resist.

**Scenario B — Xanatos Cataclysm:**
The ancient weapons recovered from Xanatos are activated, threatening mass
destruction. All factions temporarily unite to contain the threat, then
scramble to control the aftermath.

**Scenario C — The Rotting Heart Spreads:**
The corruption beneath the Ruby Citadel metastasizes, spreading through
the planet's blood-gem network. The Desolation of the Fire Dragon expands
catastrophically. Environmental collapse threatens all civilization.

### Player Activities

| Activity | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Continental diplomacy | Forge or break alliances; attend war councils |
| Mass combat command | Lead armies using simplified mass combat rules |
| Dungeon delves | Ancient sites hold keys to ending the conflict |
| Stronghold defense | Protect domain against invasion forces |
| Final confrontation | Climactic battle or ritual depending on scenario |

### Tone

Epic stakes with personal cost. The player's domain, followers, and allied
NPCs are all at risk. Victories are hard-won and pyrrhic. The ending depends
entirely on accumulated choices — there is no single "good" ending.

### Resolution

The continental war resolves. The player's stronghold survives or falls.
Allied NPCs live or die. The world settles into a new configuration based
on the player's accumulated decisions. Play continues in a post-war sandbox
at the player's discretion.
